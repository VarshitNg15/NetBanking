Transfer Guidelines

  • Instant Settlement: Transfers between NetBanking accounts settle immediately via the core Transaction Service.
  • PIN Protection: Never share your 4-digit security PIN. You can change your PIN at any time under the Accounts tab.
  • Available Balance: Transferred amounts are immediately debited from your available balance.
  • Audit Trail: Every transaction is published to the Apache Kafka transaction-events topic for real-time compliance auditing.

Multi-Factor Protected
